# Dr. Priti Malviya – Personal Website

This is a **professional bilingual (Hindi & English) personal website** for **Dr. Priti Malviya**, a PhD educator at Sandipani Vidyalaya, Bagli, MP. The website highlights her contributions to **holistic education**, cultural heritage, feminism, public speaking, and social awareness.

## Features

- **About / परिचय:** Overview of Dr. Priti Malviya's teaching philosophy, vision, and achievements in holistic education.  
- **Intellectual Work / बौद्धिक कार्य:** Articles, speeches, and essays reflecting social opinions, feminism, and cultural insights.  
- **Poetry & Prose / काव्य और गद्य:** Original writings aimed at developing students’ moral, cultural, and intellectual understanding.  
- **School Activities / विद्यालय गतिविधियाँ:** Videos and highlights of educational, cultural, and creative programs.  
- **Cultural Heritage / सांस्कृतिक विरासत:** Focus on Madhya Pradesh’s history, mythology, and traditions, linking past with modern education.  
- **Connect / संपर्क करें:** Social media links (Instagram, Facebook) and a contact form for collaborations, mentorship, and inquiries.  
- **Bilingual Content / द्विभाषी सामग्री:** Hindi for main content; English for navigation and interface.  
- **Responsive Design:** Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.  
- **Pastel Theme:** Soft, professional, clean, and visually pleasing color palette.  

## Technologies Used

- **HTML5** – Semantic structure for clean coding  
- **CSS3** – Flexbox/Grid layouts, pastel-themed styling  
- **JavaScript** – Smooth scrolling, interactive tabs, carousel for poems and videos  

## Objective

To present **Dr. Priti Malviya** as a **thought leader and brand ambassador for holistic education**, highlighting her vision of creating responsible, culturally aware, and socially conscious citizens in India.
